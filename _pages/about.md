# 👋 **Hi!** Welcome to my GitHub.io page, where I post the **potpourri** of my projects and ponderings.

👤 My name is **Derrick**! I am currently a **lab manager** for the Neuroscience of Education Research on Development <a href="https://sites.google.com/umsystem.edu/nerd-lab/home?authuser=0">(NERD) Lab</a> at the **University of Missouri** within the Department of Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences at the College of Health Sciences. Previously, I served with AmeriCorps as a paraprofessional working at a multicultural public high school in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

### 📚 **Academic Background**  
I completed my secondary education at a different multicultural public high school in **Springdale, Arkansas** before being selected as a 🏅 **QuestBridge Match Scholar** to attend 🎓 **Pomona College** in Claremont, California. My academic journey has taken me to learning from Asian Pacific Islander Desi American (APIDA) labor leaders across California to developing literacy in my heritage language at Pitzer College, Hue University, and the Southeast Asian Studies Summer Institute (SEASSI) at the University of Wisconsin - Madison. My work has been addititonally supported by the Foreign Language and Area Studies Fellowship and the Yidan Expendable Fund for Transformative Learning.

### ✨ **Passions & Interests**  
- 💡 In my free time, I love **mentoring first-generation and/or low-income students** on navigating (selective) college admissions, transitioning into college, navigating the college landscape, and transitioning post-graduation. Because college access from underserved communities is an important to me, I volunteer with a few different organizations such as *Posse* and *QuestBridge* at different capacities. I also am the creator of the WordPress.org website <a href="https://https://pomonaflischolars.us/">pomonaflischolars.us</a> where my work archiving Pomona FLI Scholars History lives.
- 📜 I'm also an artist, archivist, and advocate, preserving and curating diverse stories through different artistic archival projects—check them out under my Portfolio page! I am an artistic intellect that engages with many mediums from fiber to documentary making. I'm always crafting and creating.
- 🌍 I'm passionate about language learning and language conservation/revitalization efforts! I speak (Mexican) Spanish 🇪🇸🇲🇽 and Vietnamese 🇻🇳. Otherwise, my academic interests broadly includes Speech Perception, Phonological Representation & Meaning, Multi-sensory Integration, Psycholinguistics, and Sociolinguistics while I specifically am drawn to research about lexical tone and experimental/laboratory phonetics/phonology. I aspire to bridge linguistics with language learning for heritage speakers based on my own experience reconnecting with Vietnamese in higher education, with my ultimate dream to always analyze and research about language.
