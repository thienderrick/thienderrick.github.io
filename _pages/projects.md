### Denim Aprons #1 & #2
**Date:** Spring 2023 & Fall 2023

![My first denim apron](/images/art_Denim_Apron1and2.JPG)
#### 🔹 About the Project  
My 2D Design Professor taught another separate course Screen Printing where my friend Alicia had taken. In the screen printing room, there were these denim aprons that made me wonder... I was also taking Fiber Arts and learning about the horrifying fast fashion industry and reconciling with the massive waste of jeans and denim among many other fabrics. So, I took the thrown/donated jeans and re-purpose them into what you see today!

The second denim apron was a gift to my friend from high school who has kids now! I added pops of pink from pink denim shorts I use to own. And, here's the behind the scenes of making the second base fabric!

### Wallet from Denim Scraps
#### 🔹 About the Project  
Okay, I'm particularly proud of this one. I recycled the larger scraps of denim I had into a pattern and made a wallet out of it. An additional layer of that "woah" factor is that I re-purposed this crotch zipper from a pair of purple and indigo shorts. I think of this piece as queer art as it playfully leans into the uncomfortable unzipping of the crotch zipper to access the inside of the wallet. There's a lot of meaning here that I'm hoping to convey, even themes on monetary value and materialism.

## Writing

My writing projects are another way I explore experiences, identity, relationships, and the ways people construct meaning from their lives. Some projects are fictional, while others are more personal or experimental.

### *Hurt People Hurt People*
**Date:** In development since Summer 2026

*Hurt People Hurt People* is a long-form creative writing project about the observations and lessons I've learned in my life. This work is an autofiction novel similar to the likes of *On Earth We're Briefly Gorgeous* and draws on my lived experience to explore memory, trauma, migration, and identity formation across Vietnamese-American, queer, and first-generation contexts. Additional literature to support my creative writing journey are the following:
- *Nuevo South* by Perla Guerrero - Sociocultural Historical Analysis of Racialized Bodies in Northwest Arkansas
- *Paying the Price* by Sarah Goldrick-Rab - Institutional Analysis of Financial Aid, Higher Education, and the students trapped in the cycle of dreams and loans.
- *Insufficient Funds* by Hung Cam Thai - Cultural Economics of Migrant Vietnamese-Americans and their Families
- *Poverty, by America* by Desmond - An analysis of perpetual poverty in America from the perspective of those trapped in the system of poverty.

**About the Project:**
_Hurt People Hurt People_ follows Richard “Rich” Nguyen from adolescence into early adulthood as he moves between Arkansas, Vietnam, and elite American academic institutions, navigating queerness, Vietnamese-American identity formation, family, poverty, and belonging across the relationships and systems that shape his life. Through friendships, first loves, authority figures, and disappearances, Rich struggles to understand how harm circulates between people—not only through explicit violence, but through silence, misrecognition, institutional framing, and the limits of language across time and culture.
